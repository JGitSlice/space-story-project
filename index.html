SPACE

ADVENTURE

A mysterious signal has been detected beyond the known galaxy.

You are the captain of a damaged spacecraft drifting through deep space.
Every decision you make will change your fate as you explore abandoned stations, mysterious planets, and unknown alien lifeforms.
Will you uncover the secrets of the galaxy… or disappear into the darkness forever?

Ancient alien signals echo through the void, leading you toward forgotten worlds and hidden dangers. Your choices will shape the story, unlocking different paths, secrets, and endings along the journey. From abandoned space stations to mysterious underground ruins, every location hides something unexpected. Trust your instincts, survive the unknown, and decide the future of humanity among the stars.
